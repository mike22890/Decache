# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

## Our pledge / 我们的承诺

**中文** — 我们承诺让每个人都能在没有骚扰的环境中参与本项目，无论年龄、体型、
可见或不可见的残障、族裔、性别特征、性别认同与表达、经验水平、教育程度、
社会经济地位、国籍、外貌、种族、宗教或性取向。

**English** — We pledge to make participation in this project a
harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible
or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and
expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

## Our standards / 基本准则

**中文** — 正面示例：展现同理心与善意；尊重不同意见与经验；给出并接受建设性反馈；
勇于认错并从中学习；把社区利益放在首位。

**English** — Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

- showing empathy and kindness toward other people
- being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences
- giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- accepting responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and
  learning from the experience
- focusing on what is best for the community

**中文** — 不可接受示例：性化语言或图像、骚扰性关注；嘲讽、人身攻击；公开或私下骚扰；
未经许可公开他人隐私；传播或索取违法、侵害他人权益的内容（含侵犯著作权或规避访问
控制的内容）；其他在专业场景中不当的行为。

**English** — Examples of unacceptable behavior:

- the use of sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances
- trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- public or private harassment
- publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- sharing or soliciting content that enables violating the law or the rights of
  others, including content that infringes copyright or circumvents access
  controls
- other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope / 适用范围

**中文** — 适用于本项目的所有空间，以及个人代表本项目对外发言时。

**English** — This applies in all project spaces and when an individual
represents the project publicly.

## Enforcement / 执行

**中文** — 举报不当行为请联系维护者，举报将被保密处理。维护者有权移除、编辑或
拒绝不符合本行为准则的贡献。

**English** — Report unacceptable behavior to the maintainers. Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions
that do not align with this Code of Conduct.
